Closer

Privacy Policy

Effective 12 October 2026

The short version: Closer does not collect anything. Your deals, contacts, dates, tasks and settings are stored on your devices and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud storage. We cannot see them.

What Closer stores, and where

Everything you enter in Closer — deals, prices and commissions, key dates, the names, phone numbers and email addresses of your contacts, tasks, notes and your own signature details — is saved in the app's private storage on your iPhone, iPad or Mac. It is not uploaded to us. Closer has no accounts of its own and no servers of its own.

iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, Closer keeps your deals in step across your iPhone, iPad and Mac by saving them to the private part of your own iCloud account, using Apple's CloudKit. That storage belongs to you: it is tied to your Apple Account, it is protected by Apple, and neither we nor other Closer users can read it. If you are not signed in to iCloud, everything stays on the one device. Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

What Closer does not do

  • No analytics, crash reporting services or usage tracking
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No third-party code or services
  • No connection to anything except Apple's iCloud, and only to sync your own data

Features that hand information to other apps

  • Status updates. When you tap Send, Closer passes your draft and the recipients you chose to Mail or Messages. You review and send it there; what happens next is covered by that app and your mail or phone provider.
  • Calling, texting and emailing a contact. These open the Phone, Messages or Mail app with that contact's number or address.
  • CSV export. The file is saved wherever you choose. Once saved, it is yours to keep, share or delete.

Reminders

Date reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. They are not sent through a server.

Backups

If you back up your device with iCloud or to a computer, Closer's data may be included in that backup, in the same way as other apps' data. Those backups are controlled by you and handled under Apple's privacy policy.

Deleting your data

You can remove a single deal from its page, or everything at once with Delete All Deals in Settings; both also remove it from your iCloud storage and your other devices. Deleting the app removes its data from that device. To remove what is kept in iCloud without the app, use Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Storage on your device.

Children

Closer is a business tool for real estate professionals and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Closer changes how data is handled, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.

Questions about privacy: see the Support page for how to reach us.
