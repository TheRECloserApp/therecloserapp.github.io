Support

Answers to the questions that come up most. A contact address for anything not covered here will be added to this page before Closer is released.

How do I add a deal?

Tap + on Home or Pipeline (on a Mac, File › New Deal or ⌘N). Enter the street address and save. Add inspection, earnest money and other dates from the deal's page with Add Date.

My state doesn't say "Due Diligence". Can I change it?

Yes. In Settings › Terminology choose Option Period, Inspection Period, Attorney Review Period or Contingency Period. The new wording is used everywhere, including in status updates.

I'm not getting reminders

  • Check that Date reminders is on in Closer's Settings.
  • Check that notifications are allowed for Closer in your device's Settings › Notifications.
  • Reminders are only scheduled for dates that aren't marked done, on deals that aren't closed.
  • A Focus mode such as Do Not Disturb can hold notifications back.

How is my net commission worked out?

Price × commission % gives the gross commission. Your company's share is taken off — either a percentage of the gross or a flat fee per deal — and what's left is your net. The contract price is used once you've entered one; until then the list price is used. Set your usual figures in Settings › Commission defaults and override them on any deal.

How do I send a status update?

Open a deal and tap Send Status Update. Choose who receives it and whether it goes by email or text, edit the draft if you like, then tap Send. Your Mail or Messages app opens with everything filled in, ready for you to send.

Can I get my deals into a spreadsheet?

Yes. On the Earnings tab tap the share button, or use Export Deals as CSV in Settings (on a Mac, File › Export Deals as CSV). The file opens in Numbers, Excel or Google Sheets.

Can I switch between dark and light?

Yes. Settings › Appearance offers Dark, Light or System. Closer starts in Dark.

What are the color tags for?

Whatever helps you. Some agents tag by lead source, some by team member, some by priority. Set a tag when you edit a deal or from the deal's ••• menu, filter the Pipeline by tag, or type the color's name into search.

Is my data on my other devices?

Yes, if they are signed in to the same iCloud account. Closer syncs through your own private iCloud storage, so a deal you add on your iPhone appears on your iPad and Mac, usually within a minute. Settings › iCloud sync shows whether it is on.

Sync isn't working

  • Check that you are signed in to iCloud on each device, with the same Apple Account.
  • Check that the device is online. Changes made offline are sent when it reconnects.
  • Open Closer on both devices and give it a minute; a device that has been asleep catches up when the app opens.
  • Make sure your iCloud storage isn't full.

How do I delete everything?

Settings › Delete All Deals removes every deal, date, contact and task, on this device and, through iCloud, on your others. This can't be undone.
