Your privacy

How The Rosary handles local progress, external links and support questions.

27 October 2026 · preview

This GitHub copy

This is a GitHub-hosted copy of the pre-release privacy notice for Rosary: Pray and Learn, published by Dalton Underwood. The notice remains a draft; moving it to GitHub does not complete the outstanding privacy review.

GitHub hosts this copy and handles website requests under its own privacy statement. The separate OpenAI/Cloudflare hosting description below applies to the linked support website. Neither website hosting arrangement gives the native app a publisher-operated backend.

Privacy notice on the support website · Help and accessibility

What stays on your device

The native app has no account, advertising, analytics SDK, tracking SDK or publisher-operated server. The app does not send prayer activity, completed lessons, contacts, location or religious interests to the publisher. Prayers, lessons and images are bundled for offline use.

Saved preferences and progress

Local app preferences store learning mode, the optional Fatima prayer preference, the selected learning path, completed lessons, full-Rosary and practice-decade counts, speech speed, quiet-pause preference and the current prayer session, including its start date. The app provides no independent cloud sync. Apple device backups may include these settings according to your device settings.

Read aloud

Optional read aloud uses Apple’s device speech synthesis for displayed text. The app does not record your voice or ask for microphone access. Apple controls available voices, voice downloads and related system services.

Reset or remove your information

More → Reset saved progress clears the saved session, completion counts, completed lessons and selected learning path. Learning mode, optional prayer, speech speed and quiet-pause preferences remain adjustable separately. Deleting the app removes local app data; manage older backups in iOS Settings. The publisher cannot remotely read or erase device-only progress.

External links

Church, museum, license and Apple legal links open in your browser only when you choose them. Those websites receive ordinary web-request information and apply their own privacy notices. The app does not embed their pages or pass prayer history. Children’s mode adds an adult task before external links and leaving the mode; it is not identity verification or verifiable parental consent.

Children and families

The publisher does not collect children’s personal information through the native app. Parents and guardians should guide younger learners. An adult should contact support for a child, without sending children’s identifying details or private prayer intentions.

If you contact support

If an adult emails the publisher, the sender address, message, timestamps and technical email information are handled through Apple iCloud Mail. The message is used to answer support, privacy, accessibility or rights questions. The publisher adopted the following commitments on 27 October 2026; they are distinct from technical controls that have not yet been verified. Do not send private religious information or children’s personal data.

Adopted handling policy

Dalton Underwood is the sole routine human user under the adopted policy; necessary provider processing is separate. Routine resolved support and raw feedback must be deleted no later than 90 days after resolution, including publisher-controlled Trash, attachments and duplicate working copies. No beta audio, video or screen recordings are permitted. Necessary redacted version/scope/finding/fix/retest decisions are reviewed annually, deleted earlier when no longer needed, and capped at 5 years after supersession. A documented applicable obligation, unresolved rights matter or live dispute may justify keeping only necessary records for a justified period, with restricted access and a scheduled review.

These are owner-adopted policy commitments. Mailbox/MFA/device controls, publisher-controlled backup copies and deletion implementation have not been independently verified. Exact provider retention and processing locations remain unconfirmed; the publisher cannot promise deletion from Apple’s independent service logs, backups or TestFlight systems.

The linked support website

The publisher’s authored page files contain no analytics, advertising code, forms, remote fonts or embedded third-party media. The linked support pages are delivered through OpenAI ChatGPT Sites, with an observed Cloudflare security layer. The hosting layer adds security/challenge JavaScript, which may run in your browser and use security cookies. Cloudflare describes using browser and network signals to help identify automated requests. Technical request information can include IP addresses, browser or system information and traffic signals. These host services have their own privacy practices; this is separate from the native app’s offline progress storage. The exact linked-support-site retention and processing locations are being confirmed for the final notice.

Privacy questions and rights

Device-only progress is under your control as described above. Contact the publisher about information submitted to support, including access, correction or deletion questions. The applicable rights procedure and international processing details are being finalized before release; no universal response deadline or deletion guarantee is promised here.

Scope and updates

This notice describes the native iOS app, version 1.2.1 (8), and the support pages identified here. Apple services and external websites have their own practices. Changes such as accounts, analytics, cloud sync, purchases or new support tools require a fresh privacy review and updated disclosures before introduction.
